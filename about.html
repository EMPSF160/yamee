The antidote to commodified strategy decks.

LUME was founded in Switzerland in 2021 by veterans of tier-one global strategy houses and legendary creative design directors. We observed that modern executive counsel had been depleted of its aesthetic conviction, reduced to bureaucratic slide assemblies produced by junior analysts.

We established LUME as a private partnership dedicated to senior-partner immersion, pairing quantitative economic rigor with high-fashion brand architecture.
